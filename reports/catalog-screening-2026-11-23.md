- 01 · [Explanation-Aware Fairness Beyond Output Parity: Joint Output-Attribution Regularization for Medical Image Classification](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13052v1)。输出差异可被带符号的归因差异抵消，研究用训练目标中的联合正则项做缓解。
- 02 · [Data augmentation for the generalizability and transportability of fairness in a clinical heart failure algorithm](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.15618v1)。比较数据级干预，跨时段与跨机构结果依赖模型设计及分布差异。

- 04 · [Same Model, Different People: Numerical Execution Contracts and the Reproducibility of Fairness Audits](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.23657v1)。数值实现变化是审计变量，再入院是其中一个用例；未观察真实诊疗影响。
- 05 · [Learning through Capacity Awareness to Minimize Hospital Readmissions](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.11493v1)。干预对象是出院策略产生的后续训练观测数据，效果来自理论与仿真。
- 13 · [Paradoxical inflation of prediction model performance measures in minority patient subgroups: the role of upstream bias.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44401-026-00158-0)。人工已通过。npj Health Systems 的中科院大类分区待核。
- 15 · [Do Large Language Models Show Racial and Sex Bias in Pain Management? A Counterfactual Audit of GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, DeepSeek and Qwen](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.14935v1)。反事实病例固定临床信息并改变人口线索，未检出差异的结论受统计精度与提示设置限制。
- 17 · [Multidimensional Suicide Risk Assessment with Machine Learning using Electronic Health Records](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.03357v1)。年龄匹配重采样属于数据层干预，最年长亚组的样本量限制结论。
- 18 · [Preoperative Mortality Risk Prediction and Equity Using Preoperative Score to Predict Postoperative Mortality in a Diverse US Surgical Population: Retrospective Cohort Study.](https://doi.org/10.2196/99623)。人工已通过。JMIR Perioperative Medicine 的中科院大类分区待核。
